Website Privacy Policy

How Bloem Consulting Services collects, uses and protects personal information submitted through this website.

Bloem Consulting Services (Pty) Ltd

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Bloem Consulting Services (“Bloem Co”) collects, uses, stores, processes, discloses and protects personal information obtained through its website, electronic communications and any enquiries submitted through its online platforms.

Bloem Co is committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to its website and ensuring that all personal information is processed lawfully, fairly and transparently in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”) and any other applicable South African legislation.

By accessing or using this website or submitting personal information to Bloem Co, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and consent to the processing of your personal information in accordance with its terms where consent is required by law.

Bloem Co may amend this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version published on the website shall apply.

Definitions

For purposes of this Policy:

  • Personal Information bears the meaning assigned to it in POPIA and includes any information relating to an identifiable, living natural person or identifiable juristic person.
  • Processing includes collecting, receiving, recording, organising, storing, updating, retrieving, using, distributing, transmitting, deleting or destroying personal information.
  • Website means https://bloem-co.com and all associated webpages.

Information We Collect

Bloem Co may collect personal information including:

  • Full names;
  • Email address;
  • Telephone number;
  • Company name;
  • Position or job title;
  • Information voluntarily submitted in contact forms;
  • Any documents or attachments submitted; and
  • Correspondence between you and Bloem Co.

When you browse our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information including:

  • IP address;
  • Browser type;
  • Device information;
  • Operating systems;
  • Date and time of access;
  • Pages viewed;
  • Referring website;
  • Website usage statistics;
  • Cookies and similar technologies.

Bloem Co only collects information reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy.

How Information Is Collected

Personal information may be collected:

  • Directly from you through contact forms;
  • When you email us;
  • When you call us;
  • When you request quotations;
  • When you subscribe to newsletters or updates;
  • Through cookies;
  • Through website analytics software;
  • Through social media interactions; and
  • From publicly available sources where permitted by law.

Purpose of Collection

Bloem Co processes personal information for purposes including but not limited to:

  • Responding to enquiries submitted through our website;
  • Providing quotations;
  • Communicating with prospective or existing clients;
  • Scheduling meetings;
  • Providing consulting services;
  • Managing client relationships;
  • Complying with legal obligations;
  • Improving website functionality;
  • Maintaining website security;
  • Detecting fraud or misuse;
  • Maintaining internal records;
  • Direct marketing where permitted by law;
  • Protecting Bloem Co’s legitimate business interests.

Lawful Basis for Processing

Bloem Co processes personal information:

  • With your consent;
  • Where necessary to take steps at your request before providing services;
  • Where processing is required by law; or
  • Where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests or the legitimate interest of the data subject or another person.

Direct Marketing

Where permitted by law, Bloem Co may send information regarding its services, newsletters, updates or events.

Recipients may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting Bloem Co directly.

Bloem Co will not send unsolicited electronic marketing where consent is required unless such consent has been obtained.

Disclosure of Personal Information

Bloem Co does not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Personal information may be disclosed only where reasonably necessary to:

  • Authorised employees;
  • Professional advisers;
  • IT service providers who assist us in operating our website; and
  • Regulatory authorities where required by law.

Any third parties or service providers who process personal information on our behalf are required to maintain appropriate security measures.

International Transfers

Where Bloem Co utilises cloud-based software, website hosting providers or business applications located outside South Africa, personal information may be transferred internationally.

Bloem Co will only transfer personal information where:

  • The recipient country provides an adequate level of protection;
  • Contractual safeguards exist;
  • The transfer is authorised under POPIA; and
  • The data subject has consented where required.

Storage and Security

Bloem Co takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to safeguard personal information against loss, unauthorised access, disclosure or misuse.

Although reasonable safeguards are implemented, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Cookies

This website may use cookies to improve user experience and analyse website traffic.

Users may disable cookies through their browser settings, although doing so may affect certain website functionality.

Retention

Personal information will only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected or as required by applicable law.

Your Rights

Subject to POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion where appropriate;
  • Object to certain processing; and
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Requests may be submitted using the contact details below.

Third Party Websites

This website may contain links to third-party websites.

Bloem Co is not responsible for the content, privacy practices or security of any third-party website.

Limitation of Liability

While Bloem Co takes reasonable measures to protect personal information, Bloem Co does not warrant that its website, servers or communications are free from viruses, cyber-attacks or security vulnerabilities.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Bloem Co shall not be liable for any indirect or consequential loss arising from the unauthorised access to or interception of personal information transmitted via the internet.

Changes

Bloem Co reserves the right to amend this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always appear on this website.
