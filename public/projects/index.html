Presidential eThekwini Working Group (PeWG)

Stabilising service delivery, strengthening governance, and accelerating recovery.

Established in April 2024, the Presidential eThekwini Working Group (PeWG) is a partnership mechanism bringing together government, business and community stakeholders to stabilise service delivery, strengthen governance and accelerate economic recovery in eThekwini.

Bloem Consulting Services (Bloem-Co) serves as the PeWG's project management partner. We design the workflows and underlying systems that ensure seamless data transmission and progress loops between all relevant public and private executives.

Day-to-Day Operations

We manage the mechanism's day-to-day operations, ensuring clear accountability through structured reporting, milestone tracking and visibility of commitments across all participating partners.

Coordination Architecture

Our team developed the tools and processes that underpin coordination across the PeWG's seven workstreams, enabling improved collaboration and effective delivery.

Presidential Johannesburg Working Group (PJWG)

Addressing revenue inefficiencies, infrastructure decline, and service failures.

The Presidential Johannesburg Working Group (PJWG) was established by President Cyril Ramaphosa in March 2025 to address critical challenges facing the City of Johannesburg, including high poverty levels, revenue inefficiencies, infrastructure decline and service-delivery failures. Its mandate is to restore public confidence, prioritise essential services and drive inclusive growth.

Our appointment builds directly on our proven leadership role in the PeWG, where we developed robust multisector coordination systems and supported the execution of complex municipal turnaround strategies. Bloem Consulting Services provides strategic, technical, and coordination support to the PJWG.

Strategic Clarity & Secretariat

We provide strategic clarity and facilitate decision-making across government partners while delivering full secretariat support, including meeting management, documentation, and communication.

Accountability Tracks

We track progress across eight workstreams, escalate bottlenecks through the Presidency's oversight structures, and strengthen accountability to ensure priority actions advance efficiently.

Trust Administration

Mulilo Community Development Trusts

Refining frameworks to ensure energy investments lead to verifiable local progress.

In May 2023, Bloem-Co was appointed as the primary administrator for three Mulilo Community Trusts in the Northern Cape: the Prieska Solar Community Trust (Siyathemba Municipality), De Aar Solar Community Trust (Emthanjeni Municipality), and De Aar Wind Community Trust (Renosterberg Municipality).

Bloem-Co led a full strategic development process with Trustees, establishing a clear direction for all three Trusts. We refined the Trusts' Theory of Change and strategic framework to ensure that every investment and activity directly contributes to meaningful and measurable community impact.

Core Mandate Operations

Our responsibilities include daily administration and governance support, financial management and oversight, implementation of development programmes, and monitoring, evaluation, and reporting.

Strategic Needs Assessment

The enhanced framework enables the Trusts to prioritise effectively and align investments with their mandate. We have also initiated preparations for a comprehensive community needs assessment to support an evidence-based strategic overhaul.

Collaborative learning platforms for practitioners across the renewable-energy sector.

INSPIRE's Community of Practice is a collaborative learning platform for practitioners across the renewable-energy sector. It strengthens social-performance practice through shared learning, peer problem-solving and the co-creation of tools that improve community outcomes.

Knowledge Adaptation

Hopefield Wind Farm Local Community Company

Ensuring local communities benefit directly from renewable investments.

The Hopefield Wind Farm Local Community Company oversees community development funding generated by the Hopefield Wind Farm in the Western Cape.
